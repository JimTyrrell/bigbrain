## Licence FAQ

BigBrain is **source-available** under the PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 licence (see `LICENSE`).
You can read every line, deploy it, and earn with it in your own business. The one thing
the licence holds back is competing with what we offer.

**Can I deploy BigBrain for my own business and charge my clients while using it?** Yes.
Coaching, consulting, a membership, done-for-them programmes. BigBrain is a tool in your
business.

**Can I load it with my clients' material and answer their questions from it?** Yes. They
are your clients; that's you serving the people you serve.

**Can my VA or my web person set it up for me?** Yes. They're working for you, on your
deployment, in your account.

**I'm an agency. Can I set it up for my clients?** No, that's the one thing the licence
holds back, because it's what we offer. Talk to us about a commercial licence; agencies
are exactly who we want to partner with.

**Can I sell a course or workshop that teaches this repo?** Teaching from the public repo
is fine. Bundling the code as the product, or running a "build your BigBrain" workshop
with it, competes with ours. Ask first.

**Can I white-label it, rename it, change the look?** Change the code and the look all
you like. Just don't use our names for your product.

**Can I host it for other people?** No. Each business runs it in its own Cloudflare
account. That's the whole point of the workshop: you own it.

**Can I fork it and keep my changes private?** Yes.

**Is it open source?** No. It's source-available: you can read every line and run it,
under the one no-compete rule above.

**What about the transcripts and documents I load?** They live in your Cloudflare account
(D1 and Vectorize), nowhere else. We never see them. The seed transcripts of other
creators are deliberately not in this repo; you fetch them yourself with `npm run seed`.

**Does the licence ever change?** We can offer new versions under different terms; the
copy you have stays under the terms you got it with.

**Who decides what "competes" means?** Read the `LICENSE` file, it's a page. If you're
unsure, ask in the group. We'd rather answer than argue.
